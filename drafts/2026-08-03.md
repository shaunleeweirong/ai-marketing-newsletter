LinkedIn cracks down on AI slop, OpenAI ships enterprise agents that spooked the CX sector, and a 333-marketer survey reveals AI moved the production bottleneck — it didn't eliminate it.

## AI Use Cases of the Week

### ⭐ [70% of marketing teams deployed AI — 85% still missed a launch date (Knak survey)](https://knak.com/state-of-marketing-production/)
Survey of 333 enterprise marketing leaders (US, UK, Canada) finds 70% have deployed AI in production workflows and 64% use it for first-draft copy — but 88% say output still needs moderate-to-substantial human editing, and 85% missed at least one campaign launch date in the past year. The real bottleneck: approvals (47%) and creative production (38%), not AI capability. **Why it matters:** The best reality-check data point this week. AI is getting teams to a first draft, not to launch — the pain moved from creation to review/approval.
_Source: Knak / Datalily · Engagement: Covered by MarTech, PR Newswire, Morningstar, The Wise Marketer · Relevance: Direct_

### ⭐ [Christopher Littlestone's FOUND Framework: claimed +750% organic traffic, zero ad spend](https://foundbyaisearch.com/ai-visibility-case-study-1-found-framework/)
Restructured existing site content (no new pages, no paid ads) specifically for AI-search readability using a 5-step framework: Foundation, Optimization, Utility, Niche Authority, Data-driven iteration. Claimed result: organic traffic from 633 to 4,760 monthly visitors in 4 months. **Why it matters:** A zero-budget, structure-only GEO/AEO play — the methodology is concrete enough to steal directly for any content-led site.
_Source: Found by AI Search (practitioner blog) · Engagement: N/A (blog post) · Relevance: Direct_

### [The Search Initiative: AI Overviews optimization yields claimed +2,300% AI-referral traffic](https://diggitymarketing.com/ai-overviews-seo-case-study/)
Agency restructured a client's on-page content and schema to target citation inside Google AI Overviews. Claimed results: monthly AI-referral traffic +2,300%, 0 to 90 keywords appearing inside AI Overviews, top-10 Google keywords from 808 to 1,295 over 12 months. **Why it matters:** One of the most detailed GEO case studies published — concrete before/after methodology for teams starting to optimize for AI-generated answers.
_Source: Diggity Marketing (agency case study) · Engagement: N/A · Relevance: Direct_

### [Solo marketer builds multi-agent AI content system across 5 channels](https://www.indiehackers.com/post/we-built-this-to-create-more-content-it-ended-up-fixing-something-much-more-expensive-9e60902951)

### [WARC x TikTok: 90% of marketers use AI for creative, but only 45% say it improved quality](https://www.socialmediatoday.com/news/tiktok-report-explores-creative-ai-optimization-for-brands/825239/)
