## AI Use Cases of the Week

### ⭐ [Reddit nearly vanishes from ChatGPT citations after OpenAI search change](https://www.forbes.com/sites/gabrielalinzainescu/2026/08/20/reddit-nearly-vanishes-from-chatgpt-citations-after-openai-search-change/)
PromptWatch tracking data shows Reddit's share of ChatGPT Search citations collapsed from 3.83% to 0.52% — an 86% drop — after OpenAI shifted to using `site:` queries that target specific trusted domains directly (usage of `site:` jumped from ~0.4% to nearly 17% of queries starting Aug 8). Google AI Overviews saw a smaller 11% decline; AI Mode dropped 30%. **Why it matters:** If your GEO strategy relied on "get mentioned on Reddit so ChatGPT cites us," that play just broke. Time to diversify citation sources and monitor which domains AI models actually pull from — not just which ones rank in traditional search.
_Source: Forbes / Axios / PromptWatch · Engagement: major trade press pickup across Forbes, Axios, Search Engine Journal · Relevance: Direct_

### [10,000 marketing jobs are gone and AI agents took them](https://www.forbes.com/sites/jodiecook/2026/08/20/10000-marketing-jobs-are-gone-and-ai-agents-took-them/)
Forrester forecasts 15% of agency jobs disappear in 2026. Supporting data: 23% of agencies already cut junior copywriting roles in 2025 (31% planning more cuts), 19% cut junior design roles (24% planned), with named cuts at WPP and Omnicom. The thesis: AI agents absorbed "first draft + weekly report" work, collapsing the entry-level rung. **Why it matters:** The junior talent pipeline is thinning — senior marketers need to rethink team structure, mentorship paths, and how they develop the next generation of practitioners.
_Source: Forbes (Jodie Cook) · Relevance: Direct_

### [Salesforce: AI agents per org tripled in a year](https://www.marketingprofs.com/opinions/2026/55655/ai-update-august-21-2026-ai-news-and-views-from-the-past-two-weeks)

### [AMA 2026: marketing job postings mentioning AI nearly doubled](https://www.ama.org/marketing-news/2026-career-report/)

### [AI-generated ads show ~12% higher CTR than human-made on Meta](https://www.digitalapplied.com/blog/ai-ad-creative-benchmark-2026-ctr-roas-data)
New benchmark data shows AI-generated ad creatives outperform human-made ads by ~12% on CTR when tested on Meta at matched budget and audience, with a compounding effect from Meta's own Advantage+ AI layer. Creative-scoring data shows high-scoring AI creatives (70-100) hitting 1.84% CTR / 2.3x ROAS vs. low-scoring (<50) at 0.97% CTR / 1.1x ROAS — roughly double. **Why it matters:** The performance gap between AI-assisted and purely human creative is widening and now measurable — teams not using AI creative tools are leaving quantifiable ROAS on the table.
_Source: DigitalApplied · Relevance: Direct_

## Tool of the Week

### [PromptWatch](https://promptwatch.com/)
PromptWatch tracks which AI models (ChatGPT, Google AI Overviews, Perplexity, AI Mode) cite your content, how frequently, and — crucially — when citation patterns shift. It's the monitoring layer behind this week's biggest GEO story (the Reddit citation collapse) and gives marketers the same kind of attribution visibility for AI search that Google Analytics provided for traditional search. If your content strategy includes GEO, this is how you know whether it's working.
**Verdict:** Worth it — GEO is no longer optional for content marketers, and you can't optimize what you can't measure. PromptWatch is currently the most granular tool tracking AI citation behavior across models, and the Reddit collapse story proves why you need that data before it's too late.
_Source: PromptWatch · Relevance: Direct_
