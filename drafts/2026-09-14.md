## AI Use Cases of the Week

### ⭐ [AI-Generated Ads Beat Human Designers in Live Instagram Campaign — With a Feedback Loop](https://techxplore.com/news/2026-08-ai-generated-ads-outperform-human.html)
INSEAD and University of Wisconsin researchers built an active-learning AI system for a live outdoor-activities Instagram campaign. The AI iteratively learned which visuals drove clicks — not just which looked best. Result: 0.98% CTR vs. 0.65% for the human designer; AI won in 99.59% of samples. An 18-month follow-up (no retraining) still held. **Why it matters:** The key was the feedback loop. The AI optimized on actual click performance, not aesthetics. Build feedback loops from real campaign data into your AI creative process, or you leave the biggest gains on the table.
_Source: TechXplore (peer-reviewed in INFORMS Marketing Science) · Relevance: Direct_

### [AI Video Ads Look Identical to Human-Made Ones — and Consistently Underperform on Sales](https://hbr.org/2026/09/research-ai-generated-ads-perform-worse-than-human-made-ones-even-when-customers-cant-tell-them-apart)
HBR published research testing AI-generated vs. human-made video ads from major brands with 3,000 U.S. consumers. Consumers could not distinguish AI from human creative, but AI ads consistently scored lower on purchase intent and brand recall. **Why it matters:** The real risk is "good enough" creative that gets approved while quietly underperforming. If you use AI for video ads, do not just eyeball-approve — A/B test against human creative on actual conversion metrics. Read alongside the INSEAD study above: the difference is the feedback loop.
_Source: Harvard Business Review · Relevance: Direct_

### [A 13-Word Reddit Comment Can Trick AI Search Into Recommending Scams](https://www.tomsguide.com/ai/a-13-word-reddit-comment-can-trick-ai-search-into-recommending-scams-researchers-find)

A single 13-word comment caused ChatGPT and Gemini to recommend fake products and nonexistent businesses — with success rates as high as 100%. **Why it matters:** If your brand relies on AI search citations for discovery, you are competing with planted content. Monitor what AI chatbots say about your brand and category. "AI Engine Optimization" via authentic Reddit content is now a defensive play, not just an offensive one.
_Source: Tom's Guide / Cornell Tech · Relevance: Direct_
