# AI for Marketers — 2026-09-17

Agents everywhere this week: OpenAI opened its Agents API to the public, Google is force-migrating Search campaigns to AI Max, and the tools marketers use daily — from Copilot Studio to The Trade Desk — are quietly going agentic.

## Frontier Model Watch

### ⭐ [Google Auto-Upgrades Search Campaigns to AI Max Throughout September](https://searchengineland.com/google-sets-ai-max-migration-timeline-for-search-campaigns-485006)
Google is automatically upgrading Search campaigns using Automatically Created Assets and campaign-level broad match to AI Max throughout September. AI Max includes AI-driven search term matching, auto-generated ad copy from existing ads and landing pages, and final URL expansion — Google claims a 7% conversion lift at similar CPA/ROAS. Dynamic Search Ads sunset follows in February 2027. **Why it matters:** This is happening right now to your campaigns. Every paid search advertiser needs to audit their accounts, understand the new AI controls, and decide whether to opt out or optimise for the new system.
