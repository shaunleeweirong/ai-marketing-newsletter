# AI for Marketers — 2026-08-27

Audio and video AI hit production-grade this week — Adobe, Pika, and Runway all shipped creator-ready audio tools — while Google and Microsoft forced advertisers into AI-driven campaigns and OpenAI brought ChatGPT Ads to Europe.

## AI Use Cases of the Week

### ⭐ [SE Ranking's 2,000-article AI experiment: 97% disappeared from rankings](https://seranking.com/blog/ai-content-experiment/)
SE Ranking published 2,000 fully AI-generated articles across 20 new domains and tracked them for 16 months. Early results looked promising — 71% indexed within 36 days, 28% ranked top 100 — but by month three, only 3% still ranked, with no meaningful recovery. In contrast, human-edited AI articles on SE Ranking's established main domain earned 555,000+ impressions, 2,300+ clicks, and several top-10 rankings. **Why it matters:** The clearest public evidence that "publish-and-pray" bulk AI content is dead, but AI-drafted + human-edited content on an authoritative domain still works. The edit layer is the moat.
_Source: SE Ranking · Relevance: Direct_

### ⭐ [SteelSeries became the #1 AI-retrieved gaming brand — 23x traffic, 27x conversions](https://nogood.io/case-study/steelseries-aeo/)
SteelSeries restructured content around answer-format pages and entity signals, optimizing for how ChatGPT, Perplexity, and Gemini decide what to cite rather than chasing traditional Google rankings. Claimed results: AI search traffic grew 23x YoY, AI platform conversions up 27x, revenue up 3.7x. **Why it matters:** The first well-documented GEO (Generative Engine Optimization) case study with conversion data. AI platforms reinforce existing authoritative citations — early movers build compounding advantages.
_Source: NoGood / Shorty Awards 18th Edition · Engagement: Shorty Awards entry, covered by The Drum · Relevance: Direct_

### [SMEC audited 250+ Google AI Max campaigns: +13% revenue, but +16% CPA](https://www.searchenginejournal.com/what-smecs-data-reveals-about-ai-max-performance/568866/)
SMEC analyzed 250+ Search campaigns using AI Max. Median revenue rose 13% (close to Google's benchmarks), but median CPA also rose 16%. ROAS ranged from +42% to -35% of baseline; only 22% of campaigns hit original ROAS targets. In one unconfigured account, 69% of AI Max impressions went to competitor brand terms. **Why it matters:** The largest independent AI Max audit. The feature demands proper brand exclusion setup — unconfigured AI Max burns budget on competitor terms. The 22% success rate at original targets is a reality check against Google's marketing.
_Source: SMEC / Search Engine Journal · Engagement: European Search Awards 2026 finalist · Relevance: Direct_

### [Anthropic's marketing ops cut weekly reporting from 2 days to 2 hours](https://claude.com/blog/how-anthropics-marketing-operations-team-uses-claude-cowork-to-automate-reporting-and-campaign-builds)
Anthropic's own marketing ops used Claude Cowork to automate the weekly metrics review (claimed 2 days → 2 hours) and event campaign builds across Salesforce, HubSpot, and Swoogo. The CRM setup, marketing automation workflows, event config, and email drafting run as one sequence from an intake form. **Why it matters:** The replicable pattern: identify your most manual, multi-system marketing task and build a single AI workflow that stitches the systems together. Time savings come from eliminating tool-switching, not AI-generated copy.
_Source: Claude blog (Anthropic) · Relevance: Direct_

### [ColdIQ scaled to claimed $7M ARR with AI-powered outreach](https://x.com/itsalexvacca/article/2084322903815028763)
Alex Vacca built ColdIQ to claimed $7M+ ARR with 400+ clients using AI agents that watch 60+ data sources overnight. The model: automate targeting, timing, and messaging; keep human judgment on strategy. Shifted pricing from hourly retainers to $5K–$15K/month performance packages. **Why it matters:** The playbook for AI-native agencies — coverage goes to the machine, judgment stays with a person. ⚠️ Revenue claims unconfirmed.

### [Pangram Labs: 40%+ of long LinkedIn posts are now fully AI-generated](https://www.pangram.com/blog/ai-in-your-feed)
Analysis of 1M+ social media posts found 40%+ of LinkedIn posts over 250 words were flagged as fully AI-written. LinkedIn made up a third of content scanned but two-thirds of all AI content flagged. On X, 25% of long-form was fully AI-written with another 23% AI-assisted. **Why it matters:** If you're posting AI-generated LinkedIn content without adding original insight, you're competing with 40% of the feed doing the same thing.
