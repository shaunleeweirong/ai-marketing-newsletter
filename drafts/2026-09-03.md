## AI Use Cases of the Week

### ⭐ [Peer-Reviewed RCT: AI Email Copy Matches Human Revenue at 90% Lower Cost](https://www.chicagobooth.edu/review/2026/august/is-email-marketing-about-be-automated)
Chicago Booth researchers partnered with Wine Access, an online wine retailer, and ran a randomized controlled trial across three experiments. An AI model trained on five years of the company's most successful emails consistently matched human-written messages in revenue — but at roughly 90% lower production cost (the company's three writers earn a combined $375K/year). Published in *Quantitative Marketing and Economics*. **Why it matters:** One of the few peer-reviewed, academically rigorous studies on AI vs. human marketing copy. The insight: a model fine-tuned on YOUR past winners matters more than which model you use.
_Source: Chicago Booth Review · Engagement: Published in peer-reviewed journal, covered by MediaPost · Relevance: Direct_
