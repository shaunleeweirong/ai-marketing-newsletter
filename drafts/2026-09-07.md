## Frontier Model Watch

### ⭐ [Google Begins Auto-Upgrading Search Campaigns to AI Max](https://searchengineland.com/google-sets-ai-max-migration-timeline-for-search-campaigns-485006)
Starting September 1, Google automatically migrates eligible Search campaigns (Campaign-level Broad Match and Automatically Created Assets) to AI Max — adding AI-driven query matching, auto-generated ad copy, and final URL expansion. Existing brand inclusions and exclusions carry over, but campaigns arrive with search term matching and text customization on by default. DSA migration was pushed to February 2027 after advertiser pushback. Independent testing by Smarter Ecommerce across 250+ retail campaigns found a median 16% CPA increase, with only 22% of campaigns hitting original ROAS targets. **Why it matters:** Every paid search advertiser needs to audit their campaigns now. Review AI Max defaults before Google sets them for you — the loss of granular keyword control and auto-generated copy raise brand safety and budget-efficiency concerns.

## AI Use Cases of the Week

### ⭐ [AI Ad Creative Beats Humans on CTR — But Loses on High-AOV Conversions](https://www.digitalapplied.com/blog/ai-ad-creative-benchmark-2026-ctr-roas-data)
A benchmark study analyzing 50,000+ ad variations on Meta found AI-generated ads achieve 12% higher CTR than human-created ads. But the critical nuance: conversion rates drop 8% for purchases above $100 AOV. Below $100, AI matches or beats human ROAS. The parity threshold has risen to ~$100 by Q1 2026. **Why it matters:** The rule of thumb for any team running Meta ads — use AI creative for DTC products under $100 and for rapid variant testing. Keep human creative for high-AOV products and brand campaigns where emotional resonance drives conversion.
_Source: Digital Applied / AdBeacon · Engagement: Widely cited across PPC publications · Relevance: Direct_

### ⭐ [AI Landing Pages Beat Human Copy by 37% — With a Catch](https://www.digitalapplied.com/blog/landing-page-conversion-study-2000-pages-tested-2026)
A study of 2,000 A/B tests (all at 95% significance, 1,000+ sessions per variant) found AI-generated landing pages convert 37% better overall. But the segmented data: +3-4% on B2B SaaS and lead-gen, flat on B2B services, -2% on DTC, and -5% on webinar pages. AI-powered real-time personalization adds another 40% lift on top. **Why it matters:** AI landing pages work best for functional B2B copy. They underperform for emotional, aspirational DTC and webinar content. Use AI for the initial build, then A/B test against human variants for your specific vertical.
_Source: Digital Applied · Engagement: Cited across CRO publications · Relevance: Direct_
